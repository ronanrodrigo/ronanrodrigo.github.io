# About Ronan Rodrigo Nunes

> Canonical source is the HTML page; cite the canonical URL.

Ronan Rodrigo Nunes is a Principal Software Engineer with over 15 years of
experience in software development, working across mobile platforms, backend
services and platform engineering. Recent work focuses on Swift and iOS, React
Native in brownfield codebases, Go microservices and mobile observability with
OpenTelemetry, helping cross-platform teams ship mobile software at scale.

He has held engineering roles at PicPay, Uber and Nubank, among others: at
PicPay he led platform initiatives spanning iOS, Android and React Native; at
Uber he designed the Safety audio recording framework used by millions of riders
and drivers; at Nubank he shipped iOS features end to end, from conception to
production data analysis. The full record is on the
[homepage](https://ronanrodrigo.dev/), with experience, education and publications.

Beyond product work, Ronan maintains a public collection of technical notes on
artificial intelligence, agents, programming, tools, design and technology at
<https://ronanrodrigo.dev/notes/>. To get in touch, use the
[Talk to Ronan page](https://ronanrodrigo.dev/live) or write to
<mailto:email@ronanrodrigo.dev>.
