Privacy

This is a static personal website with no accounts, no login, no comments, no contact form and no write API. Browsing the profile, the technical notes and the resource pages requires no identification, and no personal data is requested anywhere on this site.

To understand aggregate page usage, the site uses Google Analytics (gtag UA-140410646-1) and Firebase Analytics, which may record technical navigation data such as visited pages, device type, browser and approximate region, under Google's policies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep technical delivery logs for the served pages.

Data is not shared with third parties beyond those measurement and hosting processors, and it is never sold or used for advertising. Links to external services (social profiles, cited sources, contact channels) follow each service's own privacy policy. For privacy questions, write to email@ronanrodrigo.dev.
