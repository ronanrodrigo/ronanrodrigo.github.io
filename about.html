About Ronan Rodrigo Nunes

Ronan Rodrigo Nunes is a Principal Software Engineer with over 15 years of experience in software development, working across mobile platforms, backend services and platform engineering. His recent work focuses on Swift and iOS, React Native in brownfield codebases, Go microservices and mobile observability with OpenTelemetry, helping cross-platform teams ship mobile software at scale.

He has held engineering roles at PicPay, Uber and Nubank, among others: at PicPay he led platform initiatives spanning iOS, Android and React Native; at Uber he designed the Safety audio recording framework used by millions of riders and drivers; at Nubank he shipped iOS features end to end, from conception to production data analysis. The full record is on the homepage, with experience, education and publications.

Beyond product work, Ronan maintains a public collection of technical notes on artificial intelligence, agents, programming, tools, design and technology at ronanrodrigo.dev/notes, written as discovery material with references to primary sources. To get in touch, use the Talk to Ronan page for conversation channels or write to email@ronanrodrigo.dev for asynchronous messages.
